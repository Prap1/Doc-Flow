100% Client-Side Privacy Architecture

ApniPDFs is engineered from the ground up for strict data confidentiality. Utilizing modern HTML5 File APIs and WebAssembly binaries, all computational document transformations occur locally in your browser memory (RAM). Your legal agreements, invoices, resumes, and medical records are never uploaded to remote servers or third-party cloud buckets.
